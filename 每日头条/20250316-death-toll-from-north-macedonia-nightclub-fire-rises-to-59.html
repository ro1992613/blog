20250316-Death toll from North Macedonia nightclub fire rises to 59

北马其顿夜总会火灾造成的死亡人数上升至 59 人

Note

“toll” 常见的中文意思是 “（道路、桥梁等的）通行费”；在这个新闻语境中，“death toll” 表示 “死亡人数”。

At least 59 people have been killed and more than 155 injured in a nightclub fire in North Macedonia, officials say.

The blaze broke out around 02:30 (01:30 GMT) at the Pulse club in Kocani, a town around 100km (60 miles) east of the capital, Skopje, where 1,500 people were attending a concert by DNK, a popular hip-hop duo in the country.

Note

“blaze” 在这里的意思是 “大火”“烈火”。指的是在科查尼镇的 “Pulse” 俱乐部发生的一场火灾。这场火灾大约在凌晨 2 点 30 分（格林尼治标准时间 1 点 30 分）爆发。

“concert” 常见的中文意思是 “音乐会；演奏会”。例如：“I went to a classical concert last night.（我昨晚去听了一场古典音乐会。）” 它通常指由音乐家、歌手或乐团等进行的公开表演活动，观众可以在现场欣赏音乐演出。

Prime Minister Hristijan Mickoski called it a "difficult and very sad day" for the country, which had lost many "young lives".

Arrest warrants were issued for four people, interior minister Pance Toskovski said at the scene, without giving further details. He announced the arrest of a man earlier and state news agency Mia said the club owner was detained.

Note

“warrants” 常见的中文释义为 “授权令；许可证；正当理由”。在这段文本中，“Arrest warrants were issued for four people” 里的 “warrants” 指的是 “逮捕令”，即对四个人发出了逮捕令。

“interior” 常见的中文释义为 “内部的；里面的；内陆的；内政的”。在给定的文本中，“interior minister” 可理解为 “内政部长”。

detained” 的中文意思是 “被拘留；被扣押”。在给定的文本中，“state news agency Mia said the club owner was detained” 表示国家通讯社米娅称俱乐部老板被拘留了。

Citing initial reports, Toskovski said the fire had been started by sparks from pyrotechnic devices that had hit the ceiling, which was made of highly flammable material.

Note

“pyrotechnic” 的中文意思是 “烟火的；烟火制造术的”。例如：pyrotechnic display（烟火表演）。在给定的文本中 “pyrotechnic devices” 指的是 “烟火装置”。

“ceiling” 常见的中文释义为 “天花板”“顶棚”。在这句话中，“ceiling” 指的是建筑物内部上方的覆盖部分，由于是由高度易燃的材料制成，被烟花装置产生的火花击中后引发了火灾。

“flammable” 的意思是易燃的。例如，酒精是一种易燃物质，可以说 “Alcohol is flammable.”。在给定的文本中，提到天花板是由高度易燃的材料制成，即 “which was made of highly flammable material”，强调了天花板材料容易燃烧的特性，这可能是火灾迅速蔓延的一个重要因素。

Footage shows the band playing on stage when two flares go off, after which sparks then catch fire on the ceiling before spreading rapidly.

Note

flares “照明弹”“信号弹” 或 “闪光弹”。在这段文本中，结合上下文可以理解为类似闪光弹的东西，发出强烈的光，在乐队表演时两个这样的东西被点燃，引发了后续的火花和火灾。

Video verified by the BBC shows people trying to extinguish the flames on the ceiling. The footage shows the club was still full and some people appeared to be watching efforts to put out the fire rather than leaving.

Marija Taseva, 20, told Channel 5 TV she was caught in a crush at the club as people rushed for the exits. She recalled falling to the ground and being trampled during the chaos before managing to get out.

Note

“trampled” 的意思是 “踩、践踏”。例如，人们在拥挤的场合中可能会互相踩踏，就可以用 “trampled” 来描述。在给定的文本中，“She recalled falling to the ground and being trampled during the chaos before managing to get out.” 这句话表明主人公回忆起自己倒在地上并在混乱中被踩踏，强调了当时场面的混乱和危险。

"I don't know how, but somehow I managed to get out," she told the Reuters news agency. "I'm fine now, but there are many dead."

She added that her 25-year-old sister - who her family had previously been searching for - had died, saying: "I was saved and she wasn't."

Red Cross volunteer Mustafa Saidov said most of those affected were young people aged 18-20.

"The situation is brutal, chaotic, the stories are very sad, and unfortunately many young lives are lost," he added.

North Macedonia's interior minister Toskovski said in an earlier update that 35 of the deceased had been identified at the time.

Note

“deceased” 的中文意思是 “死者”“亡故的人”。在给定的句子中，“35 of the deceased had been identified at the time.” 表示 “当时已有 35 名死者被确认身份”。

Kocani's hospital director said earlier that staff had been struggling to identify patients due to a lack of ID cards.

She said that those deceased were aged between 14 and 24. Eighteen patients are said to be in critical condition.

Local media has reported that the government is set to declare seven days of national mourning, and carry out urgent inspections of all nightclubs and restaurants used for hosting large gatherings.

Note

“inspections” 的中文意思是 “检查”“视察”。在给定的文本中，“carry out urgent inspections of all nightclubs and restaurants used for hosting large gatherings” 可理解为对所有用于举办大型聚会的夜总会和餐馆进行紧急检查。这里的检查可能涉及对这些场所的安全设施、消防设备、卫生状况等方面进行查看和评估，以确保公众的安全和健康。

Prime Minister Mickoski said the government was "fully mobilised and will do everything necessary to deal with the consequences and determine the causes of this tragedy".

DNK was formed in 2002 and has topped the charts over the past decade.

European leaders have voiced their condolences, with European Commission President Ursula Von Der Leyen saying the EU "stands in solidarity with the people of North Macedonia in this difficult time".

Note

condolences 慰问；哀悼。在这段文本中，“European leaders have voiced their condolences” 意思是欧洲各国领导人表达了他们的慰问。例如，在发生重大灾难或不幸事件后，各国领导人常常会向受灾地区或相关国家表达慰问之情。

“solidarity” 的中文意思是 “团结”“团结一致”“共同责任”。在这个上下文中，欧盟委员会主席表示欧盟 “在这个艰难时刻与北马其顿人民团结一致”，强调了欧盟与北马其顿之间在困难时期相互支持、共同应对的团结精神。

Neighbouring Serbian President Aleksandar Vucic called it "a tragedy of immense magnitude", adding that fears remain as "many more people will not be able to withstand the level of injuries they have at this moment".

Note

“magnitude” 常见的中文释义为 “巨大；重大；重要性；量级；震级” 等。在这个上下文中，“a tragedy of immense magnitude” 可理解为 “一场极其重大的悲剧”，强调悲剧的严重程度和影响力非常大。
