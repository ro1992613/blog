Feb 26 (Reuters) - On social media and at political rallies, Elon Musk has taken credit for leading the team that is cutting allegedly wasteful U.S. government spending, even waving a chain saw on stage at a conference.

“rallies” 常见的中文释义为 “集会”“群众大会”
挥舞着链锯。
“allegedly” 的中文意思是 “据称”“据说”

In court, it's a different story.

During a hearing on Monday in a case trying to block access by the DOGE cost-cutting team to Treasury Department systems, a federal judge repeatedly pressed a government lawyer to clarify who is running things. "Where is Mr. Musk in all of this?" asked U.S. District Judge Colleen Kollar-Kotelly.

"I don't have any information beyond close adviser to the president," responded Bradley Humphreys, an attorney for the Department of Justice.

attorney 律师

"You can't tell me what role he has?" the judge persisted.

Kollar-Kotelly did not say if she would block DOGE and Musk from the Treasury Department information systems as requested.

Questions about Musk's role and the activities of the team, known as the Department of Government Efficiency, are at the heart of multiple lawsuits seeking to block them from accessing government systems and slashing programs.

slashing 大幅削减

MUSK'S 'SPECIAL' STATUS

Many of the suits【案件】 allege【宣称；声称】 that Musk and DOGE are violating【违反；违背】 the Constitution by wielding【行使；运用】 the kind of vast power that only comes from agencies created through the U.S. Congress or appointments made with confirmation by the U.S. Senate.

Despite Musk's public assertions【断言；主张；认定】 to the contrary【相反的；对立的】, the Department of Government Efficiency operates in great secrecy.

政府效率部在高度保密的状态下运作。

The initial executive【行政人员；管理人员；】 orders signed by President Donald Trump setting up DOGE described it primarily as an outfit【装备；服装；机构；组织。】 to modernize software, not an agency. The Trump administration designated Musk a "special government employee."

Musk has been the face of DOGE since its inception【初始；开端；诞生。】, but White House Office of Administration Director Joshua Fisher said in a February 17 court filing【提交文件；备案文件；存档文件】 that Musk is not the outfit's administrator or even a DOGE employee.

On Tuesday, the White House identified Amy Gleason as DOGE's "acting administrator." She did not immediately respond to a request for comment made to the Trump administration.

'DELIBERATE OR NOT'

The scant information presented in court about who is ordering the cost-cutting and how it is working has led some judges to rule in favor of Musk and DOGE, at least for now.
法庭上关于谁下令削减成本及其运作方式的信息很少，导致一些法官做出有利于马斯克和DOGE的裁决，至少目前是这样。

scant【少量的；不足的；欠缺的】
rule in favor of 做出有利于…… 的裁决

To block Musk's efforts with a temporary restraining【限制；约束】 order, or TRO, plaintiffs【原告】 must show they will suffer imminent【迫在眉睫】, irreparable【无法弥补的】 harm without one, a high legal bar to clear.很高的法律门槛需要跨越。

At a hearing last week in a case brought by unions and Democratic state attorneys general, U.S. District Judge Tanya Chutkan in Washington said DOGE's lack of transparency made it difficult to show it was on the verge【边缘；濒临；即将】 of causing irreparable harm.

"I don't know if that's deliberate or not, but I will concede【承认；让步；认输】 that it is difficult to see what's happening next and to predict that and come in with a concrete【混凝土；具体的；实在的。】 harm," Chutkan said at the February 17 hearing.

The states presented her with extensive【广泛的】 news reporting about DOGE and Musk shuttering【关闭；停止运转；停止活动；】 programs, accessing confidential data, slashing funding and firing personnel【人员；职员；员工】.

"It's an understatement to say there's a lot of news reports out there," said Chutkan. "But the courts can't act based on the media reports."

She denied a temporary restraining order to prevent DOGE from accessing sensitive information at seven agencies. The case will continue and the attorneys general will seek evidence to support a more permanent injunction【禁令】.

THE GOLDILOCKS AGENCY

In some cases, however, despite noting concerns about DOGE's opacity【不透明度；模糊度；晦涩度】, judges have found there is sufficient evidence to pause its activities.

U.S. District Judge Jeannette Vargas on Friday temporarily blocked DOGE from accessing Treasury systems responsible for trillions of dollars of payments because she found there was a realistic danger that confidential information was going to be disclosed【被披露】.

She was particularly troubled that the government did not know what information was accessed by Marko Elez, a former software engineer for Musk's companies who resigned from DOGE on February 6.

"Even now, weeks after his departure【离开；出发；启程】, the Treasury Department is still reviewing his logs to determine what precisely【精确地；准确地；确切地】 he accessed and what he did with his access," Vargas wrote in her Friday ruling.

Government lawyers have even used shifting definitions【不断变化的定义】 for DOGE, which in one instance【例子；实例；事例】 worked in their favor.

U.S. District Judge John Bates in Washington denied a request by the unions for a temporary restraining order against Musk and DOGE.

In doing so【在这样做的时候】, Bates noted【注意到 / 已注意到 / 获悉 / 了解到】 that the Trump administration argued【争论；提出理由证明；主张；认为；辩论；表明；说服；争论不休；据理力争；争辩；】 DOGE was an agency in order to comply with a law known as the Economy Act that allowed DOGE to detail its staff to various government agencies.
Bates noted特朗普政府辩称 DOGE 是一个机构，以便遵守一项名为《经济法案》的法律，该法律允许 DOGE 将其工作人员分配到各个政府机构。

But Trump created DOGE as part of the Executive【行政人员；行政部门；】 Office of the President and therefore not as an agency that is subject to the Freedom of Information Act, an open records law known as FOIA.

The judge described DOGE in fairytale terms【童话般的说法 / 术语】: "A Goldilocks entity: not an agency when it is burdensome【繁重的；累赘的；难以承担的。】 but an agency when it is convenient."
一个 “金发姑娘” 实体：当它带来负担时不是一个机构，而当它方便时则是一个机构。

“Goldilocks” 常见的中文释义为 “金发姑娘”“金凤花姑娘”。在特定语境中，也可能用来形容 “刚刚好”“不冷不热、不大不小等正合适” 的状态。这里的 “Goldilocks entity” 可以理解为 “刚刚好的实体”，可能是指一种处于某种平衡状态或者具有适度特征的事物。
